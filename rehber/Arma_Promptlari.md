# Arma Yenileme Promptları

Bu dosya, 34 soylu ailenin armasını baştan çizdirmek için hazırlanmış uzun ve ayrıntılı görsel üretim promptlarını içerir. Promptlar İngilizcedir (görsel modeller en iyi onu anlar); her ailenin başında Türkçe konsept notu vardır.

## Nasıl kullanılır

- Her ailenin kutusundaki **Tam prompt** metnini olduğu gibi kopyalayın; şehir stili, aile açıklaması ve ortak stil bloğu zaten içinde birleştirilmiştir.
- Aynı şehrin tüm armalarını **aynı oturumda ve aynı modelle** üretin; böylece kalkan biçimi ve palet tutarlı kalır.
- Midjourney için sonuna `--ar 1:1 --style raw --s 150 --no text, letters, gradient, glow` ekleyin. ChatGPT/DALL·E ve Gemini için prompt'u aynen yapıştırıp ardından "negatif liste" cümlesini ekleyin. Stable Diffusion/Flux için aşağıdaki **Negatif prompt**'u kullanın.
- Sonuç çok modern ya da emoji gibi çıkarsa promptun sonuna şunu ekleyin: *"make it look like a hand-painted medieval armorial roll, with slightly imperfect linework"*.
- Kalkan dışında arka plan, çerçeve, yazı çıkarsa *"remove everything except the single shield"* diyerek yeniden isteyin.

## Ortak stil bloğu (her promptun içinde zaten var)

Medieval fantasy heraldic coat of arms illustration for a noble house, in the style of a hand-painted illuminated manuscript armorial with clean modern vector discipline: bold simple shapes, confident dark outlines of even weight (about 3-4 px at 1024 px), a strictly limited palette of 3 to 4 flat heraldic tinctures plus one dark outline colour, very subtle paper/vellum grain only in the background, no gradients, no glow, no lens flare, no sparkles, no drop shadows, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no glossy icon look. The design must read instantly at thumbnail size and still hold fine detail when enlarged. Single shield only, perfectly centred, front view, symmetrical unless the charge requires otherwise, filling about 80 percent of a square 1:1 canvas on a plain warm parchment-coloured background with a soft vignette. The shield has a thin double border (outer dark line, inner metallic line) and its field is divided or plain exactly as described. Charges are drawn in a classic heraldic manner (stylised, flat, outlined), like real medieval blazon art, not like an app icon or emoji. Do not add a crown, helmet or any ornament above the shield unless this prompt explicitly asks for it. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, mottoes, signatures or watermarks anywhere (unless a letter is explicitly requested, and none is).

## Negatif prompt (Stable Diffusion/Flux için; diğerlerinde 'şunlar olmasın' diye ekleyin)

text, letters, words, logo wordmark, watermark, signature, photo, realistic render, 3D, glossy, glow, neon, gradient, lens flare, sparkles, stars bursts, drop shadow, emoji style, flat app icon style, clip art, cartoon, anime, modern corporate logo, stock icon, busy background, multiple shields, frame mockup, cropped shield, extra limbs, deformed, blurry, low contrast, pastel colours, saturated neon colours

## Şehir sistemleri (kalkan biçimi ve palet)

- **Florentina:** Imperial capital style: a classic heater-shaped shield with a double border on EVERY Florentina shield: outer line gold, inner line crimson (not silver); NO crown above the shield. City palette: imperial purple (purpure #3B2A5E), antique gold (#D9A93B), crimson (#A6333F), ivory (#EFE6D2), outline near-black #1B1420.
- **Venture:** Merchant-republic style: a rounded-base (Iberian / Spanish) shield with a flat top and a gold bordure studded with small dots like coins. City palette: bright mercantile gold (#F2B63A), deep red (#B3262B), black (#141414), ledger green (#2F6B3F) used sparingly, outline #141414.
- **Serenquill:** Northern scholar-city style: a long pointed Norman kite shield with a silver (argent) bordure. City palette: northern azure (#2F5F8C), silver-white (#E9EEF0), deep fen green (#2F4F3A), slate grey (#5D6770), outline #14212B.
- **Aquamere:** Port-city style: a rounded 'French' shield with a subtle wavy bordure line (like a small wave pattern) in silver. City palette: sea teal (#2C6F73), deep navy (#10264A), silver (#D5DBDF), sand (#D8C49A), a touch of coral red (#B8453A), outline #0C1B33.
- **Arcan’s Gate:** Arcane-city style: a tall gothic shield with a slightly arched, pointed top (like a lancet window) and a thin silver bordure with tiny rune-like ticks (abstract, not readable). City palette: indigo (#3E3A78), midnight blue (#12162E), pale silver (#C9CCD6), a small accent of arcane teal (#4CA7A0), outline #0E0E1F.
- **Greenhall:** Frontier-forest style: a heater shield with a bordure made of a repeating oak-leaf chain in dark bronze. City palette: forest green (#2F6B3B), deep moss (#1D3F27), bronze brown (#7A5A34), bone ivory (#E8DFC6), a sparing accent of ochre (#C99A3C), outline #15210F.
